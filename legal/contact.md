## What to write about

- **Corrections.** Found an error in a resume example or a broken template? Tell us the page and what looks wrong.
- **Suggestions.** Want a guide we have not covered yet? Tell us the role and the situation you are stuck on.
- **Feedback.** Used one of our templates and got (or did not get) interviews? Both outcomes help us improve the guides.
- **Privacy requests.** For anything related to your data, mention "privacy" in the subject line so we route it correctly.

## What we cannot help with

- We cannot review your personal resume line by line. Our guides and templates are designed so you can do that yourself.
- We cannot guarantee replies to requests for job referrals or interview coaching.

## Before you write

Many questions are already answered in the FAQ section at the bottom of each guide. If your question is about a specific guide, check there first — it is the fastest path to an answer.
