# Editorial Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This page explains how Resume Path Lab creates, reviews, and maintains its guides.

## What we write about

We publish resume guides for entry-level candidates in machine learning, software engineering, and data roles. Every guide targets one real question job seekers actually search for, and answers it with complete examples, copy-ready templates, and step-by-step methods.

## How each guide is built

1. **Pick a real question.** We choose topics based on what entry-level candidates actually search for, not on what tools want to sell.
2. **Write from hiring reality.** Examples follow how recruiters and applicant tracking systems actually screen resumes: keyword matching, scannable bullets, quantified outcomes.
3. **Show, don't lecture.** Every guide includes at least one full resume or cover letter example and templates you can copy directly.
4. **No promises.** We never claim a template guarantees interviews or jobs. Hiring outcomes depend on factors no website controls.

## Accuracy and updates

Resume conventions change. We review guides periodically and update examples, tools, and terminology when practices shift. Each guide shows its last-updated date at the top. If you spot something outdated or wrong, tell us through the Contact page — corrections are fast.

## Independence

Our editorial content is not influenced by advertisers or affiliate partners. Affiliate links, when present, are disclosed on the page and in our Disclaimer, and they never change what a guide recommends. The free examples on this site are complete without any purchase.

## AI-assisted drafting

We use AI tools to help draft and edit content, and every guide is reviewed for accuracy, tone, and usefulness before publication. If something reads wrong, that is on us — and we want to hear about it.
