# Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Resume Path Lab ("we", "our", "this site") operates resumepathlab.com. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

## Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, resume files, or payment details. Like most websites, we automatically receive limited technical information when you visit:

- Your browser type, device type, and approximate location (country or region)
- The pages you visit and how long you stay on them
- The site or search engine that referred you here

## Cookies and advertising

We use cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

- **Analytics.** We may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand which guides are useful so we can improve them.
- **Advertising.** We may display ads served by third-party vendors, including Google. Third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings at adssettings.google.com. You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies at www.aboutads.info/choices.

## Affiliate links

Some pages contain affiliate links to resume-building tools. If you click one and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate partners may use their own cookies to attribute the referral. Their use of your data is governed by their own privacy policies.

## What we never do

- We never sell your personal information.
- We never ask you to upload a real resume containing your personal details.
- We never knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Data retention

Server logs and analytics data are retained only as long as needed to operate and improve the site, typically no more than 14 months.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU/UK), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you, or to opt out of certain data uses. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details on our Contact page.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
