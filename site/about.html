About Resume Path Lab

Resume Path Lab is a free resource for people writing their first technical resume.

What we publish

We publish copy-ready resume examples, bullet templates, and step-by-step guides for entry-level candidates targeting machine learning, software engineering, and data roles. Every guide includes:

  • A complete, realistic resume example you can study line by line
  • Copy-ready bullet templates with placeholders you fill in
  • A breakdown of why each example works, from a recruiter's point of view
  • Common mistakes that quietly get entry-level resumes filtered out

Why this site exists

Most resume advice is written for people who already have years of experience. Entry-level candidates face a different problem: no formal work history, only projects, coursework, and internships to work with. Generic advice does not solve that.

We started Resume Path Lab to answer one specific question well: how do you turn projects and coursework into resume proof that hiring managers actually respect?

How we write our guides

Each guide is built around a real search question candidates actually ask, then structured so you can act on it immediately. We favor concrete examples over theory, honest advice over hype, and we never promise that any template guarantees an interview.

How we make money

Some pages include affiliate links to resume-building tools such as Kickresume, ResumeGenius, and Zety. If you buy through those links, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This never changes what we recommend: the free examples on this site are complete on their own, and the paid tools are optional convenience, not a requirement.
